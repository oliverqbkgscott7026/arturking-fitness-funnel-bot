## What ArturKingFitnessBot Does

ArturKingFitnessBot organizes fitness-related communication through Telegram. Instead of depending on isolated messages or manual follow-ups, the bot leads users through a defined sequence of interactions.

The project is intended for creators, coaches, and teams building a fitness funnel in Telegram. Its central purpose is to automate that journey and deliver information and subsequent actions in a repeatable, organized way.
